## What's new in 1.1

### On-device AI with Gemma 4
Brain.md can now run Google's **Gemma 4 E4B** on your Mac. Turn it on in **Settings › Local AI & Voice**; the model downloads once (6.8 GB) and after that your notes, audio and screenshots never leave your Mac. Every answer opens in a review sheet first, so nothing changes in a note until you choose **Insert Below** or **Replace Note**.

### Skills
The ✨ menu runs **skills**: your own saved prompts, stored as Markdown files in a `Skills` folder in your vault. Five come built in (Summarize Note, Extract Action Items, Polish & Rewrite, List the Open Questions, Suggest a Better Title). Edit them like any note, or add your own from **Settings › Local AI & Voice › Instructions & Skills**. **Custom instructions** there (for example "Answer in Dutch. Keep it short.") apply to every request.

### Gemma Chat
A chat window for anything the presets don't cover: **View › Gemma Chat** (⇧⌘J) or **✨ › Chat with Gemma…**. Share the open note with one click, run a skill mid-conversation, and copy or insert any answer into your note. A gauge shows how much of Gemma's memory the conversation uses. Follow-up answers start in about a quarter of a second.

### Explain Screen
Gemma explains the slide or diagram on your screen. Capture the display under the pointer, or pick a specific window, app or display with the macOS picker.

### Meeting transcription
Record a call from the toolbar: system audio is labelled **Them** and your microphone **Me**, transcribed live on your Mac with Apple's speech engine and turned into minutes by Gemma when you stop. Supports English, Dutch (Belgium and the Netherlands) and the other languages Apple's on-device speech models cover.

### Tidier sidebar
Your notes and folders now sit under a **Notes** header, with the `Skills` and attachments folders below them under **App Folders**. Those two folders are also left out of the 3D graph.

**Requirements for on-device AI:** a Mac with Apple Silicon and about 8 GB of free memory while the model is loaded. The rest of Brain.md works as before.
