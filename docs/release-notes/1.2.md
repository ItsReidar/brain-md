## What's new in 1.2

### Choose your AI models
Pick the language model that suits your Mac in **Settings › Local AI & Voice**: Gemma 4 E4B (still the default), Gemma 4 12B, Gemma 4 E4B and E2B base models, Qwen3.5 2B, 4B and 9B, Ministral 3 3B, or Mistral 7B. Each model shows what it can handle (**Text**, **Images**, **Speech**), its size and its licence, and downloads only when you choose it. **Gemma Chat** is now **AI Chat** (⇧⌘J) and works with whichever model you use.

### Faster meeting transcription with speech models
Download a dedicated speech model for meetings: Nemotron 3.5 Streaming, Parakeet TDT v3, Orukeet, Voxtral Mini Realtime, or Whisper (Large v3 Turbo, Large v3, Small, Base). Streaming models show words while people are still talking, and every line lands sooner after the speaker stops. Without a speech model, Gemma 4 E4B listens to the meeting itself, and otherwise Apple's built-in speech engine does, as before.

### Who said what
With the small **Sortformer** model installed, Brain.md tells up to four voices on the call apart: transcripts read **Me** and **Speaker 1–4** instead of Me and Them. Consecutive sentences from the same person now join one paragraph with one timestamp, so transcripts read like a conversation, and the meeting minutes are drafted from those turns.

### A switch for all AI features
Prefer a plain notes app? **Settings › General › AI Features** hides every AI feature at once: the ✨ menu, skills, AI Chat, Explain Screen, meeting recording and the AI settings. Nothing is deleted; switch it back on and everything returns.

### Open Markdown files from Finder
Double-click any `.md` file, or use **Open With**, to open it in Brain.md. Files in your vault open as their note; files anywhere else open in the editor with a bar showing where they live, save back to the original file, and can be copied into your vault with **Add to Vault**. **Settings › General › Markdown Files** makes Brain.md the default app for Markdown (macOS asks you to confirm).

### Quick Look previews
Press Space on a Markdown file in Finder to see it rendered in Brain.md's style: headings, tables, callouts, highlighted code and images, in light or dark mode. Previews are private by design: they run no scripts and load nothing from the internet.

### Auto-save
Notes now save automatically after you pause typing, using the delay set in **Settings › General › Auto-Save Delay**.

### Fixes
- Switching notes right after typing could copy the previous note's text into the new one; it no longer does.
- Stopping a recording by switching notes now keeps the last transcript lines in the note the recording started in.
- Images inside paragraphs, lists, tables and callouts now appear in PDF exports.

**Requirements for on-device AI:** a Mac with Apple Silicon. Memory needs depend on the model you pick; Brain.md checks there's enough free before loading one. The rest of Brain.md works on any Mac running macOS 26.
