## What's new in 1.11

### LaTeX math
Write math the way you would on GitHub or in Obsidian: `$E = mc^2$` inline, or `$$ … $$` and ```` ```math ```` blocks on their own line. Formulas are drawn natively on your Mac, without an internet connection, in the preview (light and dark), in PDF exports and in Quick Look. Fractions, roots, sums and integrals, Greek letters, matrices and more are supported. Prices like "$5 and $10" stay plain text, and a formula with a typo shows its source in red so you can fix it.
