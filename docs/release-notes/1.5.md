## What's new in 1.5

### Note tabs
Keep several notes open at once. Right-click a note in the sidebar and choose **Open in New Tab**, or ⌘-click it. A tab bar appears above the editor, each tab named after its note: click a tab to switch, and close it with its **×** or ⌘W. Picking a note the usual way replaces the current tab. Your tabs are remembered per vault, so they're back where you left them when you reopen Brain.md.
