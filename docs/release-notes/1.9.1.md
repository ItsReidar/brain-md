## What's new in 1.9.1

### Fixes
- **Deleting notes in a OneDrive vault.** macOS doesn't let apps move files that OneDrive has synced to the Trash, so deleting them failed with a permission error. Brain.md now does what Finder does: it asks whether to delete the note immediately, and only does so after you click **Delete**. OneDrive usually keeps a copy in its online recycle bin. New notes that haven't synced yet still go to the Trash as before.
