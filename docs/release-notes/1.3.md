## What's new in 1.3

### Search by meaning
Find notes by what they're about, not just the words in them. Download a small search model in **Settings › Local AI & Voice › Semantic Search** (EmbeddingGemma 300M by default, 212 MB), then turn on the sparkle-magnifier button in the sidebar's search field. "budget overrun" finds the note that says "the costs exceeded the plan", and an English query finds Dutch notes too. Your notes are indexed on your Mac the first time you search, and only changed notes after that; the model unloads a few minutes after your last search, so nothing runs in the background. Smaller English-only models (from 14 MB) and larger multilingual ones are available too.

### Fixes
- Recording a meeting or using Explain Screen no longer brings up the Screen Recording request again and again. When the permission is missing, Brain.md now explains how to fix it.

### One last permission request
Brain.md is now signed with a permanent identity, so from this version on macOS keeps your permissions across updates. Because the identity changed, macOS asks one final time: for Screen & System Audio Recording, remove the old **brain-md** entry in **System Settings › Privacy & Security › Screen & System Audio Recording** with **−**, then reopen Brain.md and allow it when asked. Microphone and Documents access may be asked again once as well.
