## What's new in 1.11.2

### Faster with large vaults
- **Instant saves and file moves.** Saving, creating, renaming or moving a note no longer re-reads every note in your vault, and changes made inside subfolders in Finder or another editor are picked up right away.
- **Snappier search and Quick Switcher.** Searching by `#tag`, typing in the sidebar search bar and browsing thousands of notes in the Quick Switcher (`⌘O`) or sidebar stay responsive without stalling while you type.
- **Faster 3D Brain Graph (`⌥⌘G`).** Linking notes and laying out the 3D graph now run in the background, and spinning or filtering large graphs stays smooth.
- **Faster search by meaning and long notes.** Search by meaning scores passages using Apple's Accelerate engine, and editing long notes or meeting transcripts no longer slows down as the note grows.
