## What's new in 1.8.1

### Fixes
- **⌘⌫ deletes reliably.** Moving a note or attachment to the Trash with ⌘⌫ sometimes did nothing, for example right after launch or after opening a note from the Quick Switcher or a tab. ⌘⌫ now moves the open note to the Trash unless you're typing in the editor or in a search field with text in it.
- **Move to Trash everywhere.** Notes in search results and tag lists now have **Move to Trash** in their right-click menu too, just like the file tree.
- **No silent failures.** If a note can't be moved to the Trash, Brain.md beeps and logs why, instead of doing nothing.
