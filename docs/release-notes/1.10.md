## What's new in 1.10

### Sort your notes your way
The new **↑↓** button next to the sidebar's **NOTES** header sorts notes by **Name**, **Date Modified**, **Date Created** or **Custom**. For a custom order, just drag a note onto another to put it right above it; dragging switches to Custom, starting from the order you were looking at. Your custom order is saved in the vault (`.brain-md/sidebar-order.json`), so it syncs with OneDrive to your other Macs and survives renaming and moving notes.

### Folders or notes first
Choose whether folders or notes come first in every folder, in **Settings › General › Sidebar**, or with **Folders First** in the sort menu.
